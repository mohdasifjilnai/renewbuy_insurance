Types of Coverage offered by Motor Insurance

Explore different types of motor insurance plans to find the coverage that suits your needs.

Comprehensive Insurance Plan

Enjoy complete protection with a plan that covers your vehicle and third-party liabilities. This plan ensures you're fully covered on the road, whether it is about accidental damages, theft, or other unfortunate events.

Third-Party Insurance Plan

Protect yourself from legal liabilities with a plan that covers damages caused to others. While it doesn’t cover your vehicle, it’s a cost-effective choice for those seeking basic coverage.

Comprehensive Insurance Plan

You will be entirely covered in the case of any loss or damage when talking about comprehensive motor insurance. It is evident that comprehensive is the superior choice.

Third-Party Insurance Plan

An individual is shielded from financial damages by a third-party car insurance policy if their covered vehicle is involved in an accident resulting in property damage, bodily harm, or death to a third party.
